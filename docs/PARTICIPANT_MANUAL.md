# Participant Manual — Among Us: Live Deduction

Welcome to **Among Us: Live Deduction**! This guide explains how to use the participant portal to monitor your tournament progress, verify your scores, and view tournament standings.

---

## 1. Accessing the Participant Portal

1. Go to `/login` on any web browser (mobile or desktop).
2. Enter your participant username and password provided during registration.
3. Upon login, navigate to your personal dashboard at `/participant/dashboard`.

---

## 2. Participant Dashboard Features

### A. Personal Score Breakdown
- View your overall tournament points and preliminary ranking.
- Inspect round-by-round score cards showing:
  - Role played (Crewmate / Impostor).
  - Tasks completed.
  - Votes cast and accuracy points.
  - Survival bonus and penalties.
  - Net points awarded for each round.

### B. Score History & Audit
- Click on any round score to view its revision history.
- If a scorekeeper corrected an entry, you will see the timestamp, previous points, new points, and the recorded correction reason.

### C. IDOR Protection & Data Privacy
- You can only view your own detailed score breakdowns. The platform enforces strict server-side identity isolation; other participants cannot access your private scorecard.

---

## 3. Viewing the Public Leaderboard

- Navigate to `/leaderboard` at any time to view live tournament standings.
- The leaderboard updates automatically via real-time streaming:
  - Scores refresh instantly as volunteer scorekeepers submit results.
  - Highlights the top players advancing toward the qualification threshold.
  - Shows preliminary rounds only; practice rounds are excluded.

---

## 4. Tournament Information & Schedule

- **Event Schedule** (`/participant/schedule`): View the match schedule, upcoming round start times, and lobby assignments.
- **Tournament Rules** (`/participant/rules`): Review official tournament rules, scoring weights, conduct guidelines, and deduction policies.
- **Event Information** (`/participant/event-info`): General event details, venue announcements, and coordinator contact information.

---

## 5. Qualification & Finals

- Following the conclusion of Preliminary rounds, the top-ranked players qualify for the Finals.
- Your dashboard will display your qualification status once preliminary scoring is certified:
  - **Qualified**: You have advanced to the championship round.
  - **Did Not Qualify**: Thank you for playing! You can continue following the live finals via the leaderboard and results page.
- Once the Finals conclude and results are published, view the official championship podium and medalists at `/results`.
